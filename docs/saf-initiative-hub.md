## Overview

### Initiative Summary

`SAF` is an initiative to help SailPoint treat AI agents as first-class governed identities, not just unmanaged tools or activity sources. The goal is to give teams one place to understand what agents exist, who owns them, what they can access, what tools they use, and what actions they have taken across enterprise, browser, and endpoint environments.

### Why This Matters

As AI agents become more common, organizations need the same visibility, governance, auditability, and lifecycle control for agents that they already expect for human and machine identities. `SAF` creates the foundation for that model.

### Expected Outcomes

- A shared view of AI agents across discovery channels
- Clear ownership and access context for each agent
- Better support for audit, policy, and lifecycle workflows
- A single foundation that multiple SAF capabilities can build on
